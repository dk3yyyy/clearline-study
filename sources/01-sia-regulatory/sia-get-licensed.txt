               Introduction

The Security Industry Authority (SIA)
is the public body that regulates the
private security industry in the United
Kingdom. The Private Security Industry
Act 2001 (“the Act”) established the
SIA and sets out how regulation of the
private security industry works. Section
3 of the Act makes it a criminal offence
for individuals to engage in licensable
conduct unless they have a licence. The
SIA is responsible for granting, renewing
and revoking these licences.

applies.

assessment grid.

Licences issued
in other countries
If an Applicant currently holds a licence
to work in private security abroad and
it is a condition of that licence that
they have a criminal record check, the
applicant may send this to the SIA
as evidence of their good character.

validity and conditions of their licence,
for example, what sector it allows
the Applicant to work in and when it
expires. If the SIA is not familiar with that
licence, it will seek confirmation of the
validity and conditions from the issuing
authority in the other country. The SIA
reserves the right to request further
verification of such licences from the
Applicant.
